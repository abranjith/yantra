The 2026 FIFA World Cup[A] is the 23rd FIFA World Cup and the current edition of the quadrennial international championship contested by the national teams of the member associations of FIFA.[45]

The tournament is the first FIFA World Cup to be hosted by three countries and the first to include 48 teams, an expansion from the previous 32-team format that was used since the 1998 edition of the tournament.[46] The hosting arrangement was approved by the FIFA Congress after a competitive bidding process that concluded in Moscow in June 2018, when the United 2026 bid defeated a rival bid from Morocco during a final vote of the eligible member associations.

On June 25, 2026, total attendance reached 3,605,357 spectators, setting the record for the highest attendance in World Cup history and surpassing the previous record held by the American-hosted 1994 FIFA World Cup.[edit] Argentina is the defending champion, having won its third World Cup title in 2022 in Qatar.
